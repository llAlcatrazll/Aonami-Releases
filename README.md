# Aonami-Releases
public release version of Aonami anime tracking app

Aonami is a modern desktop anime tracking and discovery platform built with **Tauri + React** and powered by the **AniList API**.

Aonami is designed to go beyond traditional anime trackers. Instead of simply storing a watchlist, Aonami aims to become a personal anime hub where users can track what they watch, discover new series, build highly customizable profiles, visualize their anime journey, and express their identity through their library.

# 🎌 What is Aonami?

Aonami is a **personal anime platform** designed around one idea:

> **Your anime profile should feel like yours.**

Traditional trackers are excellent at maintaining databases and watchlists, but Aonami aims to make the experience more personal.

Your profile isn't just a list of anime.

It's a representation of:

- What you watch
- What you love
- How your taste changes
- Your favorite characters
- Your viewing habits
- Your anime history
- Your personality
- Your achievements
- Your personal rankings
- Your anime identity

## 🤖 Personalized Recommendations

Recommendations can consider factors such as:

- Genres
- Mood
- Pacing
- Emotional tone
- Character archetypes
- Animation quality
- Story complexity
- Studio preferences
- Previous ratings
- Watching history
- Favorite characters
- Similar anime

For example:

> Recommend anime like _The Most Notorious "Talker" Runs the World's Greatest Clan_, but with darker themes, stronger romance, and better animation.

The goal is to move beyond basic genre matching toward understanding **why someone likes an anime**.
